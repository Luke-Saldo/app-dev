# 📺 Popular TV Series Overview

## 1. Breaking Bad
- **Plot:** A high school chemistry teacher, Walter White, turns to producing meth to secure his family's future after a terminal cancer diagnosis.
- **Release Date:** January 20, 2008 – September 29, 2013
- **Major Cast:**
  - Bryan Cranston as *Walter White*
  - Aaron Paul as *Jesse Pinkman*
  - Anna Gunn as *Skyler White*
  - Dean Norris as *Hank Schrader*
  - Bob Odenkirk as *Saul Goodman*

---

## 2. Suits
- **Plot:** Mike Ross, a brilliant dropout, fakes a Harvard Law degree and gets hired by Harvey Specter, forming a powerful duo at a top law firm.
- **Release Date:** June 23, 2011 – September 25, 2019
- **Major Cast:**
  - Gabriel Macht as *Harvey Specter*
  - Patrick J. Adams as *Mike Ross*
  - Meghan Markle as *Rachel Zane*
  - Sarah Rafferty as *Donna Paulsen*
  - Rick Hoffman as *Louis Litt*

---

## 3. Lucifer
- **Plot:** Bored of ruling Hell, Lucifer Morningstar moves to LA, opens a nightclub, and helps the LAPD solve crimes while exploring his humanity.
- **Release Date:** January 25, 2016 – September 10, 2021
- **Major Cast:**
  - Tom Ellis as *Lucifer Morningstar*
  - Lauren German as *Chloe Decker*
  - Kevin Alejandro as *Dan Espinoza*
  - D.B. Woodside as *Amenadiel*
  - Lesley-Ann Brandt as *Mazikeen*

---

## 4. Prison Break
- **Plot:** Michael Scofield creates an elaborate plan to free his brother Lincoln from death row, unraveling a far-reaching conspiracy in the process.
- **Release Date:** August 29, 2005 – May 30, 2017
- **Major Cast:**
  - Wentworth Miller as *Michael Scofield*
  - Dominic Purcell as *Lincoln Burrows*
  - Sarah Wayne Callies as *Sara Tancredi*
  - Robert Knepper as *Theodore "T-Bag" Bagwell*
  - Amaury Nolasco as *Fernando Sucre*

---

## 5. How I Met Your Mother
- **Plot:** Ted Mosby recounts his journey to meeting his children's mother, sharing memorable stories of friendship, love, and life in NYC.
- **Release Date:** September 19, 2005 – March 31, 2014
- **Major Cast:**
  - Josh Radnor as *Ted Mosby*
  - Jason Segel as *Marshall Eriksen*
  - Cobie Smulders as *Robin Scherbatsky*
  - Neil Patrick Harris as *Barney Stinson*
  - Alyson Hannigan as *Lily Aldrin*
  - Cristin Milioti as *Tracy McConnell* (the mother)
